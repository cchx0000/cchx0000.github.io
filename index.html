Independent mathematical research

Arithmetic, geometry, recursion, and global structure.

My work studies how finite local data can acquire enough compatibility to force global arithmetic, geometric, analytic, or spectral consequences. Current projects span arithmetic geometry, number theory, recursive Cartan structures, geometric analysis, and mathematical physics.

Current focus Local-to-global descent; source-native arithmetic objects; recursive extension and capacity mechanisms; exact finite certificates before asymptotic or continuous readout.

01 · Research fields

Research landscape

The projects are grouped by mathematical source rather than by a single technique. A common theme is to retain enough provenance and extension data to distinguish genuine global information from a faithful re-encoding of local structure.

A

Arithmetic & motivic geometry

Prismatic and Frobenius structures, arithmetic Cartan transport, Selmer descent, mixed motives, K-theory, Arakelov-type metrics, and integral local-to-global comparison.

B

Elliptic curves & Diophantine arithmetic

Source-native elliptic families, recursive Selmer structures, BSD-facing arithmetic, rank constraints, and the relation between local extension data and global rational points.

C

Prime arithmetic & analytic number theory

Mertens and Möbius transfer, Goldbach, twin-prime and abc-type prime-birth mechanisms, RH-related finite structures, and exact additive–multiplicative compatibility questions.

D

Recursive geometry & analysis

Recursive capacity, incidence geometry, finite obstruction systems, sticky Kakeya structures, and algebraic certificates motivated by Navier–Stokes regularity.

E

Gauge theory & mathematical physics

Yang–Mills observability, gauge-invariant descent, momentum-space structure, spectral interfaces, and geometric mechanisms relevant to mass-gap questions.

02 · Common architecture

Finite structure before global readout

Across different branches, the recurring design principle is to postpone quotienting until source information, compatibility, and reconstruction have been tested exactly.

source object → recursive transport → compatible finite readouts → reconstruction / descent → global obstruction, capacity, height, or spectrum
Source-native objectsArithmetic or geometric source data remain visible long enough to retain provenance.
Recursive compatibilityLocal certificates are tracked through extension, branching, transport, and coarsening.
Exact reconstructionFinite diagrams, lattices, incidence systems, and algebraic witnesses are preferred where available.
Controlled readoutAnalytic, metric, spectral, or asymptotic quantities enter only after the finite compatibility layer is established.
