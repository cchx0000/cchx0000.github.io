02 / Framework

A shared local-to-global architecture

Across different applications, the recurring workflow is to preserve source information long enough for compatibility, descent, or capacity constraints to become visible before quotienting or passing to asymptotic readouts.

A schematic form of the program:

source data → recursive transport → compatible finite readouts → descent / obstruction / capacity

The emphasis is on exact finite structure: provenance, extension classes, filtered diagrams, incidence relations, arithmetic lattices, and certified reconstruction maps. Continuous or analytic quantities enter as readouts when the problem requires them.

Source-native objectsKeep the arithmetic or geometric object visible before compression.
Recursive compatibilityTrack how local certificates persist, split, or obstruct extension.
Finite reconstructionPrefer exact diagrams and computable finite witnesses over informal asymptotics.
Global readoutExtract a descent, rigidity, capacity, spectral, or counting consequence only after compatibility is certified.
