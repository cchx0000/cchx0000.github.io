Bio

Chenxi Cai

My current work studies how finite observer records can carry geometry, dynamics, information, and internal residues. The recurring object is an S4-completed or Regge-cutoff observer: a finite frame/readout structure whose trace, tensor, shell, boundary-area, and spinorial residues can be tested separately rather than assumed as a single continuum package.

Research

My research interests sit at the interface of mathematical physics, discrete geometry, quantum information, and finite observer theory. I am especially interested in how continuum structures can emerge from finite records, symmetry constraints, and coarse-grained readout.

Mathematical Physics

Discrete Geometry and Spacetime

Discrete and piecewise-flat approaches to geometry, including Regge-style spacetime models, finite frame structures, curvature readout, and the relation between local combinatorial data and continuum geometric behavior.

Quantum Information

Finite Records and Hidden Codebooks

Information-theoretic models in which visible finite-capacity records constrain or calibrate hidden quantum structure, with particular interest in qutrit systems, topological codebooks, and boundary-area readout.

Gravity and Astrophysics

Coarse-Grained Shell Dynamics

Coarse-grained gravitational observables, relational shell models, galactic far-field phenomenology, lensing readout, and the distinction between microscopic dynamics and retained observable shell data.

Symmetry and Representation Theory

Finite Groups and Gauge Residues

Finite group actions, S4 and related frame completions, representation-theoretic decompositions, anomaly-compatible matter ledgers, and the possibility that internal gauge structure can appear as a residue of geometric observer data.

Computation and Reproducibility

Audits, Notes, and Tools

Small computational audits, symbolic checks, static-site publishing, and reproducible workflows for turning research drafts into inspectable artifacts.

Proposes shell-quotient dynamics in which an S4 frame completion supplies a three-dimensional spin-2 readout and two-dimensional relational shells. The draft derives a logarithmic outer-disk branch, conditional BTFR scaling, and a distinct non-equilibrium merger readout.

Tensor Shell Readout from an S4 Frame Completion

Extends scalar shell theory to tensor-valued shell records M_k = Phi_k h + S_k. The trace channel remains SO(3)-protected, while finite S4 resolution splits the traceless sector into mesoscopic shear branches that average to continuum spin-2 behavior only in an orientationally disordered Regge-RG phase.

It From Qutrit: Regge Quantum Information Theory

Develops a Regge boundary information model where visible area records select a hidden qutrit codebook. The non-Clifford resource is tied to a twisted Z/3 Dijkgraaf-Witten boundary, with calibration as a finite area-only hypothesis test.
